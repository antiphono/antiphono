01 / About

A product design and research studio.

Antiphono is a product design and research studio in Sydney, Australia. We work with technology businesses on research, design, design systems and working prototypes. Operating since 2018, previously as BT Digital. The name changed because the work did.

03 / The team

Small and senior by design.

No junior layer absorbing volume and no margin taken on it.

Two contract specialists join during 2026: a senior designer, and a researcher working on applied AI tooling. Until they start, the studio is one person.

04 / The name

Antiphony is a musical term for call and response.

Two voices in dialogue, each shaping the other. That is the relationship between human judgement and AI, and it is why the name changed.
