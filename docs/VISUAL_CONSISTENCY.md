# Apex Roadster — Visual Consistency Document

Canonical design reference: `reference/apex-hero-video.mp4` (hero).
Every generated image MUST depict this same vehicle. Do not drift.

## Vehicle
- **Type:** low, wide, open-top two-seat electric roadster (concept).
- **Proportions:** long hood, cab-rearward, very low beltline, wide haunches,
  minimal overhangs, aggressive track-to-height ratio.
- **Top:** no conventional windshield — only a very low integrated wind
  deflector. Open cockpit.
- **Body:** sculptural, fluid aerodynamic surfaces; smooth front volume;
  no hard creases; clean surfacing with subtle muscle over the wheels.

## Paint / material
- Pearl-white / silver-white sculptural body, satin-gloss.
- **Black aerodynamic lower body** (front splitter, side blades, rear
  diffuser) — technical matte/satin black contrast zone.

## Lighting signatures
- **Front:** thin, curved LED light signatures (slim horizontal blades
  sweeping back). Cool white, restrained — no neon.
- **Rear:** full-width red light bar (the one restrained Signal Red accent).

## Wheels
- Large dark performance wheels (satin/graphite), aero-influenced, low-profile.

## Interior
- Minimal light-gray cockpit; two sculpted seats; clean dash; minimal
  steering interface; almost no visual clutter; tactile controls.

## Environment / camera / light
- Seamless pale-gray design studio, soft directional lighting, subtle floor
  reflection; OR dark cinematic studio for the wide/dark asset.
- Camera: premium automotive campaign language; 35–85mm equivalent; low
  heroic angles for 3/4 views; physically accurate materials.
- Finishing: restrained grade, subtle grain, cinematic but NOT sci-fi.

## Hard bans (every image)
no text · no visible brand logo · no people · no city · no neon · no cyberpunk
· no HUD · no busy dashboard · no stock-photo look
