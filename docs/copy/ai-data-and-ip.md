## H1: How we use AI, and what happens to your data

AI is part of how we work, at every stage. We think you should know exactly where, so here is the plain version.

---

## H2: Where AI does the work

Synthesising research material. Producing design and code. Generating options and variants. Writing documentation and change summaries. Analysing test and behavioural data. Keeping the project record up to date.

---

## H2: Where people do the work

Deciding what problem is worth solving. Talking to your users. Judging which direction is right. Being accountable for what ships.

Everything that reaches you has been reviewed by a person who is answerable for it.

---

## H2: We work inside your accounts

Our standard way of working is to use your AI tools, under your subscription and your settings.

Your material stays in your environment, governed by the terms you have agreed with your provider and the controls your team sets. You can see what went in and what came out, and if we stop working together there is nothing of yours sitting in our systems.

---

## H2: What that means for training and retention

Because the work runs in your account, whether anything is retained or used to improve a model is determined by your plan and your settings, not ours.

Business and enterprise plans from the major providers sit under commercial terms that prohibit training on customer material. Consumer plans generally do not, and the setting is worth checking. We will walk through this with you at the start of a project and tell you plainly where you stand.

---

## H2: Your intellectual property

Everything we produce for you belongs to you. Code, designs, research, the design system and the project record all live in your repositories and your accounts.

---

## H2: Personal data

Where a project involves personal information, including research participants and analytics, we work under the Australian Privacy Act, and under GDPR where you operate in or serve the EU.

Research participants are told how their contribution will be used, including any AI assisted analysis, and identifying details are removed before material is processed.

---

## H2: If you have restrictions

Some clients have their own rules about AI, or obligations to their own customers. Tell us and we will work inside them, including doing specific pieces of work without AI assistance where that is what your position requires.
